# Tender — browser extension

Spend crypto like cash. Tender pays your Amazon, Apple, Nike, Sephora, adidas, Zalando, IKEA and Airbnb orders straight from your wallet (ETH, USDC, USDT or SOL).

This repository holds the exact files of the extension offered on [paywithtender.vercel.app](https://paywithtender.vercel.app).

## Install

1. Download this repository (Code → Download ZIP) and unzip it, or download the extension from the website.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and pick the folder.
4. Connect your wallet on [paywithtender.vercel.app](https://paywithtender.vercel.app): the extension links itself to your account.

Works in Chrome, Brave, Edge and Arc.

## What it does

- Runs only on the checkout pages of the supported stores and on the Tender website.
- Reads the order total, asks the Tender server for a price, and every payment is approved by you in your own wallet. Tender never holds your keys or your funds.
